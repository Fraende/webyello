## Overall Impression

More thoughtful interactions than most studio sites, and the sketch really substitutes for the missing portfolio. But the page asks a wary Finnish owner to send a stranger a brief while showing no person, place or business ID. Biggest opportunity: trust and booking basics in front of the tools already built.

## What's Working

1. The sketch answers "no portfolio": honest, client-side, name and trade carry through pricing into the contact brief.
2. Quote-to-form handoff: composed brief, right dropdown option, focus to name, honest "in the package" vs "quoted on the call" tags.
3. System discipline: two-ink palette, zero-blur shadows, receipt perforation; contrast passes; reduced motion and chip focus rings handled.
